1. PROGRAMME ECONOMICS
================================================================================

  - Rs.50 bonus to the referrer on the referred friend's ACCOUNT ACTIVATION
  - 20% LIFETIME share of the brokerage generated by referred friends
  - Payout settlement: 7th of every month
  - Earnings are split into PAID (credited) and PENDING (accrued, not yet paid)

Referral lifecycle (3 stages, used consistently across all screens):

    Registered  ->  KYC Completed  ->  Activated

Rs.50 bonus is credited only at the Activated stage.

8. OPEN ISSUES - RESOLVE BEFORE / DURING BUILD
================================================================================

1. TOTALS DO NOT RECONCILE (Day 1, node 2265:14481)
   Referral Bonus Rs.2,600 + Brokerage Earned Rs.5,900 = Rs.8,500,
   but "Your Total Earnings" reads Rs.6,200. Paid and Pending sub-totals
   are likewise inconsistent. Confirm the intended formula.

2. REFERRAL CODE SPELLED TWO WAYS
   "FNI84272" (capital I) in Flow 1 and the Invalid state.
   "FN184272" (numeral 1) on every other screen.
   Confirm the canonical format and character set.

3. MY REFERRALS COUNTS CONTRADICT
   Stat cards say 120 / 80 / 40; the tab bar says All 3 / Registered 3 /
   KYC Completed 3 / Activated 3. One set is placeholder.

4. LABEL DRIFT FOR THE SAME CONCEPT
   "Brokerage Earned" vs "Brokerage Sharing"
   "Payout Cycle - 7th of every month" vs "Next Payout - 7 Oct 2026"
   "Top Brokerage Contributors" vs "Top 3 Brokerage Contributors"
   Funnel period "Current month" vs "Last 30 days"
   "Settlement on 7 Sep 2026" vs "Settled on 7th of each month"
   Pick one string per concept and centralise it.

5. WRONG SAMPLE DATA IN REFERRAL WISE BROKERAGE
   Rows for Olivia Brown and Emma Watson / Ava Wilson are labelled
   "Affiliate Sales Rs.5000" and "Referral Bonus Rs.3200" where every other
   row says "Brokerage Generated". Looks copied from another feature.

6. UNDEFINED FOURTH STATUS
   A "Pending" status pill appears on the Registered tab (Lara Michaels)
   but is not part of the documented 3-stage lifecycle.

7. STALE COMPONENT INSTANCE TEXT (do not ship)
   "High Movement" / "07 Scrips"   - leaked into referral-code sheets and
                                      several Profile rows
   "Cancel Order" / "Modify Order" - leaked into How Brokerage Works and
                                      Referral Journey sheets
   "Discard" / "Save Changes"      - leaked into How it Works
   "Add Bid", "EVENT", "8 orders"  - leaked into Profile
   These are unoverridden design-system defaults, not content.

8. FLOW 1 INVALID STATE
   Button still reads [Verify] while the error is shown. Confirm whether it
   should be disabled, or stay active for retry.

9. SETTLEMENT DATE INCONSISTENCY
   "Settlement on 7 Sep 2026" (Brokerage) vs "Next Payout 7 Oct 2026"
   (Referral Journey). Both claim to be the next payout.

10. FLOW DECISION OUTSTANDING
    Three referral-code capture flows sit in a page marked "Ready to Dev".
    Flow 2 (collapsed link -> sheet) is least disruptive to organic signups;
    Flow 3 (dedicated screen) is most aggressive. Confirm before building.
